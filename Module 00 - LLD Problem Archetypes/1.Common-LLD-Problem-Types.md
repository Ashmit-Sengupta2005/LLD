# Common LLD Problem Types (Archetypes)

> **Not part of the course series** — kept here because almost every LLD question asked to freshers is one of these eight shapes. Recognise the shape, and you already know the entities and the patterns before you start designing.

---

## 1. The 8 archetypes, by how often they're asked

| # | Archetype | What it is | Frequency | Problems |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | **Commerce** | Browse → cart → pay | **Very high** | Shopping Cart, Flipkart, Amazon, Swiggy, food ordering |
| **2** | **Allocation** | A pool of things; assign one, release it later | **Very high** | **Parking Lot**, BookMyShow, Library, Hotel booking, Car Rental |
| **3** | **Lifecycle** | A thing moves through fixed stages | **High** | Vending Machine, ATM, Elevator, Order tracking, Traffic Signal |
| **4** | **Game / Board** | Turns, moves, win check | **High** | **Tic-Tac-Toe**, Snake & Ladder, Chess |
| **5** | **Ledger** | Money moving between accounts | **High** | **Splitwise**, Digital Wallet |
| **6** | **Fan-out** | One event, many listeners | **Medium** | Logger, Notification service, PubSub |
| **7** | **Matching** | Pair two sides | **Low** | Uber, ride booking, delivery dispatch |
| **8** | **Content + Subscription** | Catalog + who may watch it | **Low** | Netflix, Spotify, YouTube |

> **⭐ The top 5 cover roughly 90% of fresher LLD questions:** Commerce, Allocation, Lifecycle, Game, Ledger.

---

## 2. What each archetype gives you for free

The moment you recognise the shape, you already have your entities and your likely patterns.

| Archetype | Core entities | Patterns that usually fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Commerce** | `Product`, `Cart`, `CartItem`, `Order`, `Payment`, `User` | **Strategy** (pricing, discounts, payment method), **Factory** (payment objects) |
| **Allocation** | `Resource` (slot/seat/room), `Booking`, `User`, `Manager` | **Strategy** (allocation rule, pricing), **Singleton** (the manager), **Factory** |
| **Lifecycle** | `Machine`, `State`, `Transition`, `Request` | **State** (the whole point), **Strategy**, **Observer** |
| **Game / Board** | `Board`, `Cell`, `Player`, `Piece`, `Move`, `GameState` | **State** (game state), **Strategy** (`WinStrategy`), **Factory** (pieces) |
| **Ledger** | `User`, `Group`, `Transaction`, `Split`, `BalanceSheet` | **Strategy** (split/fee type), **Observer** (notifications) |
| **Fan-out** | `Event`, `Publisher`, `Subscriber`, `Channel` | **Observer** (the whole point), **Chain of Responsibility** (log levels) |
| **Matching** | `Rider`, `Driver`, `Trip`, `MatchingEngine` | **Strategy** (matching rule), **Observer** (status updates) |
| **Content + Subscription** | `User`, `Content`, `Subscription`, `Plan` | **Strategy** (plan rules), **Decorator** (feature tiers) |

> **The pattern that shows up most: Strategy.** Any time a requirement says *"this can be done in several ways"* — pricing, splitting, allocating, winning — that's an interface plus one class per way.

---

## 3. Where to spend your time

**Study the top 5 properly.** Matching and Content+Subscription are usually asked as **HLD** ("design Netflix for 200M users"), not as a coding design. If one does come up as LLD, the standard entity breakdown plus Strategy carries you through.

**Self-study list (not covered in the course):**

| Problem | Archetype | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Splitwise** | Ledger | Asked constantly; Strategy falls out naturally (see Module 10, Lecture 3) |
| **Shopping Cart** | Commerce | The most common archetype of all |
| **Tic-Tac-Toe** | Game / Board | Neither fits the other archetypes — needs its own practice |
| *Snake & Ladder* | Game / Board | Optional — same shape as Tic-Tac-Toe, with dice |

---

## 4. Quick Recall

- **Identify the archetype first** — it hands you the entities and the likely patterns before you design anything
- **Top 5 = ~90% of questions:** Commerce, Allocation, Lifecycle, Game, Ledger
- **Allocation** (parking lot, booking) and **Commerce** (cart) are the two most likely to come up
- **Lifecycle problems → State pattern.** **Fan-out → Observer.** **Anything "several ways to do X" → Strategy**
- **Matching and Content** are usually HLD questions, not LLD
- **Practise:** Splitwise, Shopping Cart, Tic-Tac-Toe
